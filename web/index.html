About your data

Model: . OpenRouter and its selected model provider receive your conversation context.

Exa receives search queries and requested URLs. Its keyless access has rate limits. Each new chat uses one small extra model request to generate its title. There is no Finrod conversation backend or automatic request replay.

Your key is stored in this browser until you forget it. The key and history are not application-encrypted. Anyone with access to this browser profile or malicious website updates could read them.
